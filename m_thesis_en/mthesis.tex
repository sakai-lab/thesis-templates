\begin{coverabstract}
This document demonstrates the updated English master's thesis template using
an illustrative study of information access systems. It contains a research
question, a proposed evaluation procedure, example tables, a mathematical
definition, and a discussion of limitations. All numerical results are invented
for typesetting purposes. The journal and conference references are also
explicitly fictional examples, rather than evidence for the statements below.

The layout separates chapter-opening pages from continuation pages. Each chapter
begins on a fresh page with a centred page number and no running header. Later
pages retain alternating chapter and section headers. Chapters may begin on
an odd or even page, so no empty page is inserted merely to reach a right-hand
page. The first chapter includes two continuation pages to make both running
header positions visible. The remaining chapters contain a continuation page
for comparison. These demonstration page breaks can be removed when writing
an actual thesis.

The example also demonstrates a table of contents,
cross-references, acknowledgements, a bibliography and an appendix. Together,
these elements provide a practical document for reviewing margins, heading
hierarchy, caption placement and continuous page numbering.
\end{coverabstract}
